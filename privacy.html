Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

1. Information We Process

When you connect your TikTok account to the Service, we access, with your authorization, your TikTok user profile (open ID, display name, avatar) and your ability to upload and publish videos. We may store tokens provided by TikTok to allow the Service to publish on your behalf.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information solely to provide the Service: to identify your account, to upload and publish content that you instruct the Service to publish, and to provide status and history of your scheduled publications.

3. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens are stored locally and used only to interact with the TikTok platform. We do not sell your personal information. We take reasonable measures to protect stored data, but no transmission or storage method is fully secure.

4. Third-Party Platforms

Your use of the Service involves the TikTok platform. Your data shared with TikTok is governed by TikTok's own terms and privacy policies.

5. Your Rights

You may disconnect your account at any time by revoking access through TikTok or the Service. When access is revoked, we will no longer be able to act on your behalf.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the Service operator at the contact information provided on the Service website.
